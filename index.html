      How to read this dashboard

      • Overview answers "what is it all worth and how is it doing?"
      • Holdings lists every position, who owns it and which account it is in.
      • Activity is the list of purchases and sales that everything else is built from.
      • Allocation shows what the money is spread across.
      • Risk shows how bumpy the ride could be and which holdings drive that.
      • The filter row at the top changes every number on every tab. Press the small i next to any figure for an explanation.

      What is simulated in this demo

      • The family (Alex, Sam and Jo), their positions and what they paid.
      • Every price, the exchange rate, dividend yields and a year of price history. Tickers are real so you can see how yours will look, but the numbers are not.
      • Anything you import or add is kept in this browser only. The live site will save it for the whole family.
      • If you import a symbol that is not one of the sample tickers, the demo invents a price for it, starting from your latest purchase price, and guesses its currency from the ticker.
      • Cost in pounds for US holdings uses today's exchange rate. The live site can record the rate on the purchase date.

      Feeding in a portfolio

      Keep one spreadsheet with a row for every purchase or sale, then press Import activity sheet. The first row must be headings. Four columns are needed and four are optional:

      ColumnNeeded?What goes in itExample
      DateYesThe day of the trade. Day first, or year-month-day.12/03/2024
      SymbolYesThe Yahoo Finance ticker. London shares end in .LAAPL, SHEL.L
      QuantityYesNumber of shares or units.25
      PriceYesPrice paid per share, in pounds or dollars.171.50
      TypeNoBuy or Sell. Blank means Buy.Buy
      OwnerNoWhose holding it is.Alex
      AccountNoISA, SIPP, GIA and so on.ISA
      CurrencyNoGBP, USD, or GBX if the price is in pence.GBX
      For information only. This is not financial advice. Rules of thumb shown here are general guides and may not suit your circumstances.
